# FNO spectral pre-check: is the residual representable within k_max?

Generated by `audit_port/scripts/33_fno_spectral_precheck.py`, n = 200 ground-truth trajectories from the frozen sampler. **Run before the FNO-with-baseline cells were written**, so its answer cannot have been chosen to suit a result.

## The question

Adding the IEC baseline makes FNO's spectral branch represent `theta_TO - H` instead of `theta_TO`. FNO truncates at `k_max = 16` of 50 modes. `H` carries the first-order relaxation — the dominant low-frequency content — so removing it takes away exactly what the truncation keeps. If the residual's energy sits above mode 16, that cell would measure mode truncation and not delta-learning.

| signal | energy above mode 16, median | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| `theta_TO` | 1.68e-02 | 2.75e-02 | 5.48e-02 |
| `theta_TO - H` | 4.64e-03 | 1.89e-02 | 5.05e-02 |

## Endpoint mismatch — J-90's other prediction

J-90 predicted FNO pays Gibbs ringing at the window edge because the FFT treats the window as periodic while `theta_TO(0) != theta_TO(T)`. The residual is zero at `t = 0` by the IC mask, so if `H` tracks the endpoint the residual is closer to periodic and the penalty should fall.

| quantity | median | p90 |
|---|---|---|
| `\|theta(T) - theta(0)\|` | 11.122 degC | 29.687 degC |
| `\|resid(T) - resid(0)\|` | 0.471 degC | 1.602 degC |

Amplitude the branch must represent falls from 16.05 degC peak-to-peak to 0.90 degC, a factor of 17.9.

## Verdict

**The residual is representable within `k_max`.** Median energy above mode 16 is 4.64e-03, under the 5% limit set before measuring. The FNO-with-baseline cell therefore tests delta-learning, and a poor result there may be read as evidence about delta-learning rather than about the truncation.
