# Is gas NMAE usable on the realistic benchmark?

Generated by `audit_port/scripts/28_gas_nmae_floor.py`. Ground truth by RK45 over the 720 min window at 50 points, 100 cases per benchmark. **No model is involved** — the normalisation denominator is a property of the ground truth, so this holds for every model scored on these sets.

`eval/benchmark.py` normalises by per-case peak-to-peak with a floor of 0.0001. When the floor binds, the reported percentage is `MAE / 0.0001` — a rescaled absolute error wearing a percent sign, carrying no information about the case it came from.

## The denominator against what a practitioner can measure

The floor is not the only way a normalisation loses meaning. Even where the denominator clears 1e-4 it can still be an excursion far below anything an instrument resolves, and a ratio between two such numbers is well defined and physically empty. ## Reading

The realistic sampler did not break the gas metric; it exposed how little the gases move. Consistent initial conditions start each unit near its own gas equilibrium instead of up to 30 degC away from it (audit M-9), so the 12 h transient that the old sampler's mismatched ICs produced is gone. The gases genuinely barely change over half a day — that is the physics of `k_gen V_arr / k_dis` against a 12 h window, not a modelling failure — and normalising by a quantity that is almost zero is what manufactures the large percentages.

This is the same defect the audit found at 34,558% on acetylene, now arriving from the other direction: there it inflated a monolithic baseline's error, here it would flatter or distort any model's. Neither number means anything.

## Consequence

**Gas NMAE is not reportable from this benchmark.** Quote absolute ppm against the IEC 60599 thresholds in `cod/eval/metrics.py`, which is what C-9 already makes the primary metric. The percentage columns for the five gases should not appear in the paper at all — not as a secondary figure, not in an appendix — because a reader cannot tell which entries are ratios and which are `MAE / 1e-4`.

`theta_TO` is unaffected: its variation is of order 10 degC and the floor never binds, so thermal NMAE remains meaningful.
